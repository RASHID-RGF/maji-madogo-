# Portfolio framing notes

## Audience
The primary audience is program and operations leaders at the Ministry of Agriculture and regional water agencies who need a clear, evidence-based way to prioritize infrastructure investment and maintenance spending.

## Tone
The writing is concise, practical, and outcome-driven. It focuses on decisions, trade-offs, and measurable impact rather than technical detail for its own sake.

## Story angle
This project is not about building a dashboard for the sake of a dashboard. It is about turning operational water data into a decision-making tool that helps direct resources where they matter most.

## Evidence strategy
Screenshots are selected to prove three claims:

1. the data quality and operational picture were established,
2. the most critical service gaps were identified,
3. the recommendations translate into measurable operational value.

## Why this works
A recruiter or stakeholder can scan the README and immediately understand the problem, the analytical method, and the business consequence without opening a notebook.
